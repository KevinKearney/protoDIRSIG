# Backlog

The single to-do list for protoDIRSIG. Add an item any time; remove it when done or dropped (git history is the record). Each item is a `###` title and a short body, so the file imports into GitHub issues one to one; the group heading becomes the label.

Group **TBR** holds items whose resolution may require a change on the MANIFOLD side (run-spec schema, guides, registry, sensor-spec). protoDIRSIG records them and does not edit MANIFOLD documents itself.

### Three resolution roots, one rule
Engine assets resolve against `manifold_config_repo/`, the sensor ref against `manifold_sensors/`, motion and tasks are generated. A run spec may carry the sensor as a ref or inline; which form the registered descriptor carries is C-21.

### Strict loader and canonical hashing (MANIFOLD registry side)
Unknown keys are rejected at admission (the run-spec schema) and canonical-JSON hashing (RFC 8785) gives run ids, both SDK-side; duplicate-key rejection is not built (the loader is plain `yaml.safe_load`, which keeps the last value), and whether the registry's hash equals the run id is MANIFOLD's (C-12). `content_hash` is the sha256 of file bytes (`scripts/stamp_hashes.py`), verified when stamped; a `.scene` ref is not stamped, since geometry and materials sit beside it. Replace the stamp with the registry's hash.

### Compile-once
`submit` then `run()` runs `scene2hdf` twice. Irrelevant for one job, material for sweeps.

### `run_info.json` is not a complete plugin record
`plugin_list` lists only `NewAtmosphere` and `BasicPlatform`; `SpiceEphemeris` and `ThermWeather` are missing. Do not use it as the provenance record of plugins.

### `--threads` in `engine.run`
Partly addressed: a run can now request a thread count through the run spec's `resources.cpu_threads` (`built / proposed`, `tests/test_resources.py`; DIRSIG honors it with `--threads`; it enters the run id when stated, C-30), and the engine block itself still has no thread member (C-08). The determinism probe's reason stands: at the default thread count a demo's radiance can differ between runs of one spec, and one thread made every probed repeat bit-identical (3.1 to 3.7 times slower; `docs/DIRSIG_determinism.md`). Decide with MANIFOLD whether the request belongs in `resources` or `engine.run` (C-08, C-28, C-30).

### Space-to-space: what the first measurements leave open
From `docs/Space_to_space_characterization.md` (round 29), recorded as gaps, none closed by extending the spec. (1) The sampling of an unresolved target is not a run-spec member: DIRSIG needs a surface behind the object and the `::IMPORTANT::` label (scene content) and its hit quantum is set by the template's hypersampling multiplier and the convergence settings (neither a member); at multiplier 100 the R⁻² law fails below about a third of a pixel area. (2) Why `--convergence` does not change a pixel whose rays all leave the scene is not isolated. (3) Target motion is scene content (the family's moving plates, fixed speeds); a parameter would need a member. (4) The shutter-blur axis is unmeasured: an unresolved streak is undersampled at this multiplier, and a hyperresolved moving plate is not in the family. (5) An Earth background, stars and a PSF are absent. (6) The residuals: E1's integrated signal rises to 1.008 of the expectation at 100 km; the pixel-centred 1.2-pixel plate peaks at 0.903 of a full pixel where the geometry gives 1.000. (7) SatSim: nothing run (C-38).

### Space-to-space: what round 30 leaves open
From `docs/Space_to_space_characterization.md` (round 30, family II), recorded as gaps, none closed by extending the spec. Round 29's items (2) and (6) are explained there: the earth core ends a pixel's sampling at its first path to it, E1's residual is seed-to-seed sampling scatter, and the 0.903 peak is DIRSIG5's aperture-dependent spread. Item (4) is measured. (1) Platform motion at a stated speed has no member: the `orbit` form's speed is the TLE's and it tracks its LookAt point, so blur from platform motion is expressed by moving the scene (one speed per scene instance). (2) `::IMPORTANT::` hypersampling does not follow a moving object over the exposure, so an unresolved streak loses flux as it lengthens (0.25 of the expectation for a 49-pixel streak); the multiplier and the sample count are not members. (3) The earth core and `--disable_earth_core` are not members; every target and background needs a surface behind it. (4) A surface about 20 km along the ray inside the generated scene was not hit; mechanism not isolated. (5) The resolved blur length is 1.7 to 3.1 percent short of speed × exposure over the footprint at 5 and 20 ms, for target and background alike; unexplained, and `integration_samples` has no effect (DIRSIG5 ignores it). (6) DIRSIG5 applies an aperture-dependent spread (about 0.18 pixel σ for `auror-nir`) without a `<psf>`; the sensor-spec has no PSF member, and the family specs' `fidelity.absent` "optics blur" entry is inaccurate (not regenerated: run ids). (7) An Earth background beside a target is not expressible (one `engine.scenes` entry); stars and SatSim remain (round 31).

### Space-to-space: what round 31 leaves open

## Sensor model

### Real QE and optics data
Import the Teledyne SCION curve with `scripts/import_curve.py` (`--provenance vendor_typical` or `measured`, `--pad-zero-to 0.150,14.000`; its ~0.3-1.7 um range does not cover the job's 0.41-2.0 um bandpass), point `deepscan_850_306_nir_1280.yaml`'s `qe_reference` at it, update `qe_peak`, and restamp. State in `--source` whether the quoted QE includes the die window or microlens. Likewise `synthetic_silicon` and `synthetic_vis_lens` if a real VIS sensor replaces the invented one.

### Received AUROR_ref radiometry carries a 0.399 factor
Its native gaussian channel peaks at 1/√(2π) (CONOPS section 9). Decide whether `auror_ref.yaml` stays `native` (reproduces the tree) or moves to `tabulated` (unit peak) and its reference renders are regenerated.

### Generator scope
One sensor entry, one focal plane, one `settings` member per job (refused otherwise at resolution); several channels per focal plane are generated. Not generated, and refused when a sensor-spec asks for them: a non-identity mount (needs the quaternion-to-`<mount>` convention, and `axis_convention` is unconfirmed), lens distortion, a mosaic `channel_layout` (DIRSIG `<channelpattern>`), a rolling shutter (DIRSIG `detectorarray@rollingreadout`, seconds per line, which the sensor-spec does not carry), a `timestamp_reference` other than `exposure_start`. Not written and not refused: `AdcBitDepth` (DIRSIG quantizes only in `<detectormodel>`, which also needs full well, read noise and dark current, none of them modeled), image flips (`x/yflipaxis`; no sensor-spec field), and `DeviceVendorName`/`DeviceModelName` (no DIRSIG element; platform metadata is free text). Instrument and focal plane names, truth collections, spatial response (PSF) and hypersampling stay as the template has them. `throughput_in_band` is checked against the optics curve only in the tests. Beam-split optical paths and `.platform` noise models are not generated.

### `split_channels: true` with `new_atmosphere`
Refused at resolution: the AUROR database has no per-channel spectral states, and the render fails after the dry run passes. A database built for the per-channel states would lift it; `tests/test_sensor_render.py` flags when the render succeeds.

### Spectral grid
Synthetic curves are 1 nm over 0.150-14.000 µm; imported curves keep their source grid. Channels are tabulated on the template's 0.41-2.0 µm bandpass; a sensor needing the UV or LWIR needs a template with that bandpass and scene and atmosphere data to match.

## SDK and run-spec engine

### Sweeps and layers: what remains after the sensor axis
Sweeps exist over the sensor axis only (CONOPS §3.5). The space-to-space families show the gap (round 32): their axes (range, exposure, look angle, Sun, offset, speed, phase) are geometry and settings, not sensors, and their specs are generated outside the composer so that they have no id-snapshot entry, so `scripts/s2s_measure.py` runs them one at a time through `Simulation.run` or `Workspace.submit_run` (byte-identical images) instead of `submit_sweep` (`docs/Space_to_space_characterization.md`). Remaining: a grid over other axes (exposure, epoch, geometry), with grid versus zip decided per axis and the run cap applied to the product; a compiled-scene cache keyed by scene hash, so the runs of a sweep that share a scene compile it once (with compile-once); and splitting scene-specific from engine-general engine-profile content, now that the AUROR and VIS profiles are one file with a recipe override.

### SDK API: after phase 2b-3 (`api/`)
`api/` states the SDK API (`sdk-api/1`, `built, partial / internal`: no REST server or `RemoteBackend` yet, `tests/test_local_backend.py`); `Workspace` over `LocalBackend` implements every operation for the local case, and what departs from the contract is listed in `api/operations.md`. `LocalRegistry` is deprecated (a DeprecationWarning points to `Workspace`) and is removed in phase 3, once the notebooks use the facade. The run-spec schemas leave some members open (`collection.platform`, `field`, `settings[].binning`; engine `motion` kind `waypoints`, the `four_curve` and `basic` atmospheres, `weather.source: install`) until the MANIFOLD vocabulary or a run defines them. Re-execution of an identical spec stays out of `sdk-api/1`.

### SDK API: the REST server and `RemoteBackend` (phase 3)
A REST server over `LocalBackend` (each call returns the wire form already) and `RemoteBackend`, a client generated from `api/openapi.yaml`, run against `tests/backend_conformance.py`; download URLs for artifact references.

### `LocalBackend` on Windows and other non-POSIX hosts
The run store and the worker use `fcntl` locks, `/proc` and process groups (`start_new_session`, `killpg`), so `LocalBackend` runs on POSIX hosts only. A port needs another lock, a process-liveness check and a way to terminate a worker's children.

### Authoring friction (round 35)
Measured in `docs/Authoring_friction_audit.md` (`tests/test_friction_audit.py` recomputes each count). The shortest path (a shipped recipe as a dict, window edited) writes 0 files, 2 fields and 0 hashes; the items below are what lies off it.
- **Hashes outside a checkout.** An engine profile carries 3 to 6 `content_hash` fields (`tahoe_static_pose` 4, `tahoe_leo_pass` 6, `pointcollectors2_demo` 3), a sensor 0 to 2, a pass-through recipe 1. `scripts/stamp_hashes.py` writes them only in the repository's own three libraries (no path argument), and no SDK call writes one; with the installed packages alone the user computes each (`hashlib`, `manifold.core.dirhash`) and types it: 4 for a new profile like `tahoe_static_pose`.
- **The wrong-hash refusal shows 12 of 64 hex digits** of the right hash, so it cannot be pasted back (1 message).
- **File locations.** A recipe file outside `<library>/recipes/` does not compose (its layers resolve against the file's grandparent; the same recipe as a dict does). A run-spec file finds its sensors in `manifold_sensors/` beside its folder's parent, so one elsewhere needs `sensor_library=`: all 121 committed space-to-space specs, passed as files.
- **Swapping the sensor of `auror_ref` takes 3 edits**, not the 2 the CONOPS sensor path names (`sensor`, `entry_id`): its `engine_overrides` (`channel_response: native`) refuses the other two library sensors' tabulated curves.
- **A missing settings member is a Python error.** Without `exposure_time`, `frame_rate`, `gain` or `black_level` (4 members), the refusal is `library_files`' `platform could not be rendered from the sensor-spec: KeyError: '<member>'`, not a schema problem naming the field.
- **The black-level refusal says "use 0 or omit it"**; a settings member without `black_level` is refused (the `KeyError` above): 1 message whose advice is not accepted.
- **`recipes/leo_pass_tahoe.yaml`'s `fidelity.approximated` is 4 items, not 3**: the flow list's `Earth orientation (UT1 - UTC, no polar motion)` splits at the comma. Fixing it changes the recipe's run id and its id-snapshot entry.
- **The libraries ship with a checkout only.** `Workspace.local()`'s three default roots (`library`, `config_repo`, `sensor_library`) point into the checkout (`workspace.REPOSITORY`); none of the three libraries is in a distribution.
- **A space-to-space spec by hand** is 72 fields, 8 of them copied (3 hashes, the generator revision, 3 asset names, `library_entry`); the committed ones are written by `scripts/s2s_scenarios.py`.
- **The product direction** (a form or GUI that writes recipes and layers, a CLI, or neither) is a decision for Kevin (CONOPS section 10.1). `scripts/recipe_form.py` prototypes the recipe step only: an offline page that writes the 20 required recipe fields from the library's listings, with no SDK change.

### Partial artifacts of a cancelled DIRSIG run
Since round 21 the worker handles the SIGTERM of a cancel: it stops the engine's process group, runs the adapter's collector and lists what it reports (SatSim's frames, the dummy engine's), with the verdict `incomplete`. Left: DIRSIG's collector reports nothing without the runner's `engine_result.json`, so a cancelled DIRSIG run still lists only `run_spec.json`, although a pass-through run's outputs are in its folder; a collection mode that reports whatever exists, with roles, is a change to the adapter protocol in `manifold.core` and to the DIRSIG adapter. Also: the backend waits 10 s for the worker, so a cancelled run whose collection hashes many large files is cut off and lists only `run_spec.json`; a size-aware bound would fix it.

### dirfm writes an explicit zero scene offset
dirfm's jsim states every scene as `{"inputs": ..., "offset": "0,0,0"}`; a demo's own simulation file states none. ### The scene compile's flags
dirfm runs `scene2hdf` with `--threads <cpu count>` and the run's seed; the pass-through runner runs it with neither. Round 28 (`docs/DIRSIG_layered_comparison.md`): for VertexNormals2 the compiled scene changes both files, and the layered job's folder re-compiled without the two flags (with the pose's rounding and the zero offset restored) gives the same-seed pass-through digests; for ChipMaker2 the same re-compile restores the image but the truth's `Dominant Optical Property Index` band differs (the index a compile assigns), and only the pass-through run's own compiled scene gives both digests. Fix: compile the layered job's scene with the pass-through runner's flags (or state the compile's threads and seed in the spec); it changes the bytes of affected layered renders (not run ids). The truth index numbering needs its own look (a compile-to-compile difference, not a flag).

### The epoch is written in UTC
dirfm's `.tasks` writes the reference time in UTC (`...T17:00:00.000000+00:00`); a demo writes the same instant with its local offset (`...T12:00:00.0000-05:00`). Visible-band renders are unaffected; thermal ones are not: Balfour2's layered render equals the same-seed pass-through render with only that string restored, DataDrivenTempSolver1's with it and the Euler angles (round 25). Mechanism not isolated (a hypothesis: the temperature prediction reads the time of day in the written offset). Decide whether the run spec's epoch keeps its offset (`descriptor.collection.epoch` is stated with an offset; the loader converts it to UTC) or the scene's local offset is derived from its origin; either changes thermal results, so it needs a stated rule and C-n question. Round 26: MappedTherm1 (with the demo's `.tasks` and angles) and Thermal2 (with the demo's `.tasks` and without the zero offset) the same way, both thermal. Round 28: TemperatureMap1 the same way, the reference time's text alone (thermal).

### Throttle rule and idle cores

## dirfm upstream (out of scope here; candidates for issues or patches)

### NewAtmosphere is unusable as shipped
Six attributes are never initialised under `_FrozenAttrs`, and `"Isacc"`/`"Distort"` are typos for `"Isaac"`/`"Disort"`. Worked around in `atmosphere_patches.py`; `tests/test_atmosphere_patches.py` flags an upstream fix.

### Plugin plumbing
`SPICEPlugin` requires three kernel paths. `PlatformSensorPlugin.prepare()` always regenerates platform, motion and tasks. `write_files()` assumes a `PlatformSensorPlugin` (`UnboundLocalError: platform_plugin_idx` otherwise). Worked around in `platform_ref.py`.

### Missing wrappers
`EarthGrid`; STK-report import (`source="stk_report"`); a quaternion orientation engine; `LookAt` with a velocity-tracking `up`; a native SGP4 location engine.

### Numeric formatting
`PlatformPosition.add_entry` writes angles with `{:0.6f}` (π becomes 3.141593) and positions to 3 decimals. This changed every pixel by ~1e-7. Suggested fix: `repr` or `{:.17g}`.

### Dead and silent paths
`DIRSIG.set_output_prefix` stores `_prefix` and nothing reads it. `SCENE._check_coverage` passes vacuously on a `_fname` scene and looks like a pass.
